Helpful starting details

  1. What problem are you trying to solve?
  2. What happens today, and what have you already tried?
  3. Who will use or approve the solution?
  4. What would a successful result look like?
  5. Is there a deadline, and what makes it important?
  6. Do you have a budget range or procurement constraint?

You do not need all the answers before contacting us. We will clarify what is still unknown.

What happens next

  1. We review the problem and identify the key unknowns.
  2. If the request is a fit, we propose a clear next step—sometimes a focused assessment before a larger build.
  3. You receive written scope, deliverables, exclusions, acceptance criteria, timeline assumptions and commercial terms.
  4. Work starts only after the proposal and terms are approved by both sides.
