Security & data handling

Protect the work. Limit unnecessary exposure.

Security requirements vary by project. We discuss the data involved, the access required and the controls that are appropriate before work begins.

Access and credentials

  • Access should be limited to the systems and permissions needed for the agreed work.
  • Credentials and tokens should not be committed to source repositories or included in client-facing evidence.
  • We agree how production access is granted, reviewed and revoked at handover.
  • Client environments and information are handled separately to reduce accidental cross-project exposure.

AI-assisted work

AI-assisted tools may be used for bounded engineering tasks when appropriate. Client code and confidential information are not sent to free or unapproved AI services. Restricted information—such as credentials, personal records and live financial data—is not processed through AI workflows. We do not treat model output as proof that software is correct, secure or ready to release.

Where a project has stricter requirements, AI use and permitted processors should be agreed before relevant information is shared. Ask us for the proposed processing approach during scoping.

Testing and release

We select checks based on the technology and consequences of failure. Depending on the engagement, these may include automated tests, dependency review, static analysis, browser testing and release verification. We report the checks actually performed and any known gaps; this page is not a certification or independent security audit.

Incidents and vulnerability reports

If you believe a Company OS website or project has a security issue, contact us through the channel below. Include the affected page or system, the observed behaviour and a safe way to follow up. Do not send passwords, access tokens, personal records or live financial data in an initial report.

Report a concern on WhatsApp

We will review the report and coordinate next steps. A specific response-time or notification SLA is not promised by this public page; project-specific obligations must be agreed in writing. For automated disclosure discovery, see our security.txt.

Data, retention and handover

Data location, subprocessors, retention, deletion, backup handling and any cross-border processing depend on the systems used for a particular engagement. These details should be identified before sensitive information is transferred and documented in the project agreement where applicable. At handover, we agree the transfer or revocation of access and the disposition of project materials within our control.

We can discuss confidentiality terms and a written data-handling arrangement before work begins.
