Finance operations · Traceability · Exceptions

Know which records match—and which do not.

Make reconciliation repeatable with explicit matching rules, visible exceptions and evidence behind every resolution.

The problem

Manual matching is hard to audit and harder to scale.

Payment exports, bank statements, invoices and internal records may describe the same activity using different references or timing. A useful process distinguishes a confirmed match from a likely match, an unmatched item and an unresolved data problem.

Capabilities

Designed for explainable matching

  • Import and normalize approved source files or system feeds.
  • Apply agreed matching rules using reference, amount, date and validated fields.
  • Detect duplicates, missing records, amount differences and timing differences.
  • Route ambiguous or unmatched items to a human review queue.
  • Record evidence and decisions associated with each exception.
  • Produce period summaries and drill-down reports for operational review.

Rules are agreed against the client's actual data and risk tolerance. No match-rate guarantee is made before representative data has been assessed.

Typical deliverables

What the client receives

Source and rule map

Source inventory, field mapping, matching precedence and known data-quality limits.

Reconciliation workflow

Repeatable imports, matching outcomes and an explicit exception path.

Evidence and reports

Traceable references, period summaries and documented review/approval steps.

Controls

Financial correctness is the boundary

  • Source records remain preserved; corrections are recorded rather than silently overwriting history.
  • Automated matches use deterministic rules agreed with the client.
  • Ambiguous items are not forced into a match to improve a dashboard.
  • Access to restricted financial data is limited to approved systems and people.
  • Cut-off times, tolerances and exception ownership are explicit.
