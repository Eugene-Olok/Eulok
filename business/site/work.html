The engagement

Understand first. Agree clearly. Deliver with evidence.

A straightforward process designed to reduce surprises, make decisions explicit and leave clients able to operate what they own.

From problem to handover

  1. Understand. We learn what is happening today, who is affected, what has already been tried, and which constraints matter. If the problem is unclear, we recommend a bounded assessment rather than guessing.
  2. Define the scope. We document the desired outcome, deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, risks and acceptance criteria. Unknowns remain visible until resolved.
  3. Agree the proposal. You receive the delivery approach, timeline assumptions, milestones and commercial terms. Both sides approve before implementation begins.
  4. Build and review. Work is delivered in reviewable increments. We test relevant behaviour and discuss material scope changes before proceeding.
  5. Verify and accept. Deliverables are checked against the agreed criteria. We report what was tested, known limitations and any unresolved issues.
  6. Handover and support. We provide the agreed code, documentation, operating instructions and access-transfer steps. Ongoing support is defined separately, including its scope and response window.

Designed to prevent avoidable surprises

Scope stays visible

New requests are assessed and approved as changes; they are not silently added to an existing agreement.

Failures are first-class

Integrations and automations account for timeouts, duplicates, delayed responses and ambiguous records—not only the happy path.

Money-moving logic gets care

Financial workflows preserve traceability and send ambiguous matches or consequential corrections for review.

Ownership is explicit

Source, access, documentation, limitations and ongoing support are addressed in the engagement terms.

What we need from you

Useful starting information includes the problem, what you have tried, who approves the work, what success means, the deadline and the available budget range. Partial answers are fine; we will identify what remains unknown.

Commercial terms

Depending on the work, we may propose a fixed-scope assessment, a fixed-price build or milestone-based delivery. Payment timing, acceptance, change control and any deposit are stated in the proposal. Support or availability beyond the agreed scope is not implied.
