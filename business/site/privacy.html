Privacy

A clear account of website data.

This notice describes the information that may be processed when you visit this website or contact Company OS. Project-specific terms may add to it.

Who is responsible?

Company OS is the contact point for questions about information submitted through this website. Contact us using the WhatsApp link below and identify the page or enquiry concerned. This notice does not itself establish regulatory registration or certification status.

Information you provide

If you contact Company OS through WhatsApp, the information you choose to send—including your contact details, project description and any files—is processed by WhatsApp/Meta as well as being available to the recipient of your message. Please do not send passwords, payment credentials, sensitive personal records or confidential production data in an initial enquiry.

We use enquiry information to respond, assess fit, prepare a proposal and manage a potential engagement. We do not operate a contact form on this website.

Website logs and analytics

The hosting provider may process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the site. Optional PostHog analytics can be enabled by deployment configuration. When enabled, analytics may process page views, referrer and campaign information, browser/device details, and an IP address or derived approximate location according to the analytics provider's behaviour and settings. The repository's default analytics configuration is empty; this does not by itself establish the configuration of a live deployment.

We do not intentionally send project data, contact-message contents or payment records to website analytics. Analytics settings and provider behaviour can change; ask us if you need confirmation about a particular deployment.

Cookies and third-party processing

When analytics is enabled, the analytics service may use cookies or similar browser storage. Hosting, analytics and messaging services may process information in countries outside Uganda. Their own privacy notices and terms also apply. We do not claim that this website uses only local data processing.

To reduce analytics collection, use browser privacy controls or tracker blocking. If analytics is enabled on a deployment, browser controls may not be equivalent to a site-level consent preference.

Retention and your requests

Enquiry information is retained only as needed for the conversation, proposal, engagement, legal obligations and legitimate recordkeeping, subject to applicable law and any project agreement. Technical and analytics retention may be controlled by the hosting or analytics provider and its configuration; this notice does not promise a fixed deletion period for every system or backup.

You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you provided, or to ask a question about its handling. We may need to verify your request and retain some records where required by law or a legitimate contractual obligation.

Ugandan data protection

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and to assess applicable obligations under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act and other relevant law. This notice is not legal advice and does not represent a claim of regulatory registration or certification. Applicable registration, lawful-basis, cross-border transfer, retention and breach-reporting obligations should be assessed for the actual processing taking place, including website analytics and project work, with the Personal Data Protection Office or qualified Ugandan counsel where needed.

Contact about privacy

For privacy questions or requests, contact Company OS and state that your message concerns privacy.
