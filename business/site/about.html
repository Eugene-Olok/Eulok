About Company OS

Engineering for the work businesses depend on.

Company OS is a software engineering company based in Kampala, Uganda, working with organisations locally and remotely to build, connect and improve the systems behind their operations.

Our point of view

Software should make important work clearer and more dependable—not add another layer of uncertainty. We start with the business problem, understand the existing workflow, and choose the smallest solution that can meet the need.

Sometimes that means building a new system. Sometimes it means connecting tools that already work, repairing a fragile application, or proving that a proposed rebuild is unnecessary.

What clients can expect

Clear commitments

Scope, deliverables, exclusions, dependencies and acceptance criteria are agreed before implementation begins.

Visible trade-offs

Risks, unknowns and decisions are explained in plain language. Material changes are discussed before they affect cost or delivery.

Verification, not theatre

Testing and review are matched to the system and its risks. We distinguish what has been checked from what still needs attention.

A usable handover

Deliverables include agreed documentation, known limitations and the access or operating guidance needed to continue responsibly.

Where we focus

Our strongest fit is operational software: integrations, payment and record reconciliation, workflow automation, software rescue and practical business systems. We also build fast, accessible websites for organisations that need a clear and credible online presence.

We work with clients to verify provider requirements, data sensitivity and any relevant regulatory obligations before making implementation commitments. We do not claim accreditation or certification we do not hold.

How we work

We use AI-assisted tools where appropriate, while keeping responsibility for scope, review and delivery with accountable people. Client information is handled according to the agreed sensitivity and processing boundaries; sensitive or restricted information is not sent to unapproved AI services.
