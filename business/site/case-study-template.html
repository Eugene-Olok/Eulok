Evidence standard

Case Study Template

This page describes the structure used for future approved case studies. It is not a client story and makes no claim about completed engagements.

Required structure

  1. Client context. Industry and operating constraints, disclosed only with permission.
  2. Original problem. The workflow or failure to address, supported by an agreed baseline where relevant.
  3. Approved scope. What the engagement included, what was excluded and which dependencies mattered.
  4. Delivered work. What was implemented, including important design and engineering decisions.
  5. Verified outcome. Results measured against a documented method, with limitations and evidence provenance.
  6. Lessons and ownership. What changed, what remains, and what the client owns or maintains.

Publication gates

  • The engagement is complete and acceptance evidence exists.
  • The client explicitly approves the exact material to be published.
  • Every quantitative result has a reproducible source and a stated measurement window.
  • Client data, credentials, private architecture and unrelated personal information are excluded.
  • No fabricated testimonials, logos, metrics or capabilities are introduced.

Current publication status
