Accessibility

Usable across devices and ways of interacting.

We aim to make this website understandable, navigable and usable for people with different access needs. Automated checks are useful, but they cannot establish full accessibility conformance on their own.

Our approach

  • Use semantic HTML, meaningful headings and page landmarks.
  • Provide keyboard-visible focus and a skip link to the main content.
  • Respect reduced-motion preferences and support narrow screens.
  • Use readable contrast and avoid relying on colour alone to communicate important information.
  • Test pages with automated browser checks and address reported barriers.

Assessment status

The site has automated browser and accessibility checks in its engineering workflow. These checks do not replace manual testing with keyboard-only navigation, screen readers, zoom and real user feedback. We have not established a complete independent WCAG 2.2 AA conformance assessment, so we do not claim full conformance.

Report an accessibility barrier
